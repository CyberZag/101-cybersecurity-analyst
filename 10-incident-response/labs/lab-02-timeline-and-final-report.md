# Lab 02 — Timeline and Final Report

**Type:** Lab Guide / Simulated Case Study (home-lab VMs)

## Objective
Build a chronological timeline from the Lab 01 scenario's telemetry and write a full incident report.

## Prerequisites
- Completed Lab 01.

## Steps
1. Pull timestamps for every relevant event across your log sources (process creation, network connection, any authentication events).
2. Merge them into a single chronological timeline (a simple table: timestamp | source | event | host/user).
3. Decide and document a containment action appropriate to the scenario (e.g., isolate the host, disable the account) and your reasoning.
4. Write the final incident report with: executive summary, scope, timeline, containment/mitigation actions, and lessons learned.

## Expected outcome
A complete simulated incident report with a clear timeline and justified containment decision.
